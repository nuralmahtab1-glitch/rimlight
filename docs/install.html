Rimlight Install Guide

Make your screen edges glow with your music. Free, for Windows 10 & 11. Takes about 2 minutes.

1. Download

1

Open this link in your browser:

https://github.com/nuralmahtab1-glitch/rimlight/releases/latest

2

Under Assets, click Rimlight-win-Setup.exe to download it (about 90 MB).

If your browser asks, choose Keep.

2. Install

3

Open your Downloads folder and double-click Rimlight-win-Setup.exe. No admin password needed.

4

If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.

This shows up because Rimlight is a small free app that isn't "code-signed" yet. It's safe.

5

Rimlight installs in a few seconds and starts by itself. A thin glowing line appears around your screen. 🎉

Says "Smart App Control blocked this"? That box has no "Run anyway" button. To install, turn it off: Start → type Smart App Control → open it → choose Off. Your antivirus stays on. (Note: on most PCs it can't be turned back on without resetting Windows.)

3. Find it in the tray

Rimlight has no big window: it lives in the system tray, bottom-right next to the clock.

6

Click the small ^ arrow next to the clock and look for the rounded-square icon with a glowing pink/purple outline.

7

Tip: drag that icon out of the ^ menu onto the taskbar so it's always visible.

4. Try it

8

Play a song in Spotify (or YouTube, Apple Music, any music app). Within a second the glow takes the colors of the album cover and moves to the beat.

Want to change things? Click the tray icon to open the settings, or see the separate "How to Use Rimlight" guide.

Updates

Rimlight updates itself. When a new version is out you'll get a little notification, and it installs the next time Rimlight starts. You don't need to download anything again.

Uninstall

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → find Rimlight → ⋯ → Uninstall.

Is it safe / private?

Yes. Rimlight never uses your microphone and never records anything. It listens to the sound your PC is already playing, analyses it in memory and throws it away. No account, no ads, no tracking. The only internet use is checking for updates and (if lyrics are on) looking up the song name on the free LRCLIB lyrics database.
